# 🌟 Kazakhstan IELTS - English & IELTS Coaching

## 🚀 About Kazakhstan IELTS
Kazakhstan IELTS is a local English and IELTS coaching program offering structured group and 1-on-1 lessons, transparent tuition pricing, free practice exam materials, and real student feedback.

---

## 🎯 Key Features
✅ **General English & IELTS Prep** – Structured group and individual lesson plans.
✅ **Transparent Tuition & Fee Calculator** – Clear per-class pricing with an interactive cost calculator.
✅ **Free Practice Materials** – Reading and Listening mock exams included with tuition.
✅ **Student Reviews** – Real feedback from current and past students.
✅ **Modern & Responsive UI** – Works seamlessly on all devices.

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack
🖥️ **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js
💾 **Storage:** No backend required
🎨 **UI/UX:** Intuitive and user-friendly design

---

## 📦 Installation & Setup
1️⃣ Open `index.html` in your preferred web browser.
2️⃣ Explore tuition pricing and the fee calculator via `Kazakhstan_IELTS_course_tuition.html`.
